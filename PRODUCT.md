# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 static export, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Cloudflare Pages branch previews.

## Users

Founders and hiring leads evaluating Jasher for global remote AI Automation Engineer, Automation Lead, and early engineering roles. Consulting prospects are secondary visitors.

## Product Purpose

Help a time-poor evaluator understand Jasher's role, strongest systems work, and evidence in one visit, then download a public-safe resume or make contact.

## Positioning

An operator-turned engineer who builds AI automation around approval boundaries, recovery, and proof that the intended outcome actually landed.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive between interviews or meetings and need a credible picture in under a minute. They can scan the hiring dossier first, then inspect public, anonymized, and historical case studies at their own depth.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static portfolio with home, work index, case-study, experience, and notes routes.
- No forms, database, client-side secrets, tracking additions, or external write paths.
- Resume downloads are direct and public-safe; existing resume filenames remain reachable.
- The current production site and custom domain remain unchanged until a separate release approval.
- Cyndra appears only through public role facts and safe anonymized operating lessons.
- No client data, private screenshots, client logos, invented outcomes, or unverified metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Lead with `AI Automation Engineer · n8n · Claude/OpenAI · Agent Systems`.
- Keep Jasher's real portrait in a supporting role.
- Personal identity remains distinct from True Hub Solutions, Cyndra, and Lady.
- Voice is grounded, exact, technically capable, and never an AI-guru persona.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public GitHub repositories for Claude Agent Patterns, THS WhatsApp Intake, and Claude Max API Proxy.
- Verified local and vault records for receipt-bound agentic runtime work and historic OpenClaw work.
- Public Cyndra role information.
- Existing real portrait and resume assets in `public/`.

## Product Principles

- Proof before claims.
- Make the hiring decision easy in one visit.
- Distinguish public, anonymized, and historical work clearly.
- Explain system limits as honestly as system strengths.
- Let the work lead, with personality in the evidence and writing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, retain full keyboard and screen-reader operation, keep content available without animation, support reduced motion, and prevent horizontal overflow on common phone widths.
